Human-decided merges for duplicate publications that no identity key can safely unify (different coverDate/creator formats across sources, missing DOIs). Approved merges are durable: full rebuilds re-apply them, and incremental syncs cannot resurrect the retired duplicate. Rejecting a request suppresses future suggestions of the same pair.

Direct merge
The DUPLICATE is folded into the SURVIVOR (edges re-keyed and deduplicated, missing fields backfilled) and then deleted. Without "rebuild now", projections are marked dirty for the Conflicts-page rebuild. Reversing an applied merge requires flipping the decision to rejected and running a full derived-data rebuild.
